ARCHIVAL EXHIBIT • LETTER 902

Vincent's Last Letter

Auvers-sur-Oise • 23 July 1890 — Found in Vincent's pocket after his death

AUVERS VG 1890
POSTE • 23 JUIL 90 AUVERS-SUR-OISE

Auvers-sur-Oise, 23 Juillet 1890

Mon cher frère Theo,

Merci de ta bonne lettre et du billet de 50 fr. qu’elle contenait. En ce qui me concerne, je m’applique à mes toiles de tout mon cœur; j'y risque ma vie et ma raison s'y est effondrée à moitié...

“Well, my own work, I am risking my life for it and my reason has half-foundered in it — that's all right — but you are not among the dealers in men as far as I know, and you can still choose your side, I think, acting with humanity... What's the use?”

“Well, indeed, we can only speak through our paintings.”

Je te serre la main bien cordialement en pensée,

Vincent

POSTES 1890 50 c
AUVERS 23 JUIL 90
VINCENT VAN GOGH • 1853–1890
